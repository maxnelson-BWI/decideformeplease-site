// faq

You have questions. I have rulings.

→Are you actually qualified for this?

Not even a little bit. But I’ll give you a straight answer, and something to show the other person that ends the argument. That’s worth ten bucks.

→Will you settle our serious argument?

No. If your fight involves lawyers, real money, or anyone’s actual wellbeing, that’s above my pay grade, and my qualifications are zero, remember. I rule on the small stuff: chores, names, plans, food opinions, and who’s right about the thing from 2019 you’re both still mad about. If you send me something serious, I’ll refund you and gently point you toward a real professional.

→What if we disagree with the ruling?

That’s the entire point. You couldn’t settle it, so I did. My ruling is final. No appeals, no rebuttals, no ‘yeah but what about…’

→Do both of us get the verdict?

Only if you add their email — then they get it the moment you do, no softening it in the retelling. Skip it and the ruling is yours alone, to screenshot and deploy however you like.

→Isn’t hearing one side unfair?

Wildly. I account for the fact that you wrote the story. Testimony that’s suspiciously flattering to the author tends to lose.

→Is this AI?

No. It’s a real person reading your actual argument and making an actual call. That’s the whole product. Not an algorithm. Just some guy with an opinion and zero fear of confrontation.

→What kinds of arguments will you settle?

Tiny, low-stakes stuff: chores, road-trip seating, whose turn it is, the right way to load a dishwasher, dumb recurring debates. The smaller the argument, the happier I am.

→Is this a joke?

Real service. Real money. Real human. Also genuinely funny. Those things are not mutually exclusive.
